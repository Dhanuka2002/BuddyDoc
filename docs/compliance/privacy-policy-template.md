## Introduction

PatientPrep SL ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

**IMPORTANT:** PatientPrep SL is designed with privacy as our top priority. All your health-related information (symptoms, medications, notes) is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with anyone.

---

## Key Privacy Principles

### 🔒 Your Health Data Stays on Your Device
- All symptoms, medications, and health notes are stored only on your device
- We cannot access, view, or retrieve your health information
- Your health data is encrypted and secured locally
- Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all your health data

### 📊 Minimal Data Collection
- We only collect non-health data necessary for app functionality
- No personal health information is transmitted to our servers
- All data collection is transparent and with your consent

---

## Information We Collect

### Information Stored ONLY on Your Device (We Cannot Access)
- **Symptoms and Health Concerns:** All symptom logs and descriptions
- **Medication Information:** Your medication lists, dosages, and schedules
- **Questions for Doctors:** All questions you prepare for consultations
- **Appointment Details:** Your scheduled appointments and doctor information
- **Consultation Notes:** Any notes you take after medical visits

### Information We May Collect (With Your Consent)
- **Account Information:** Email address for app login (encrypted)
- **App Usage Analytics:** How you use app features (no health data included)
- **Device Information:** Basic device type and OS version for app compatibility
- **Crash Reports:** Technical information to fix app issues (no personal data)

## How We Use Your Information

### Health Data (Local Only)
- **Storage:** Encrypted on your device only
- **Access:** Only you can access this information
- **Usage:** Solely for your consultation preparation
- **Sharing:** Never shared with anyone, including us

### Non-Health Data (With Consent)
- **App Improvement:** Understanding which features are most helpful
- **Technical Support:** Fixing bugs and improving performance
