# Problem Statement

## Core Challenge
Many patients struggle to effectively communicate their health concerns during a doctor's consultation, leading to missed information, hurried visits, and potential misdiagnosis.

## Patient-Side Problems

### 🧠 Forgetfulness
- Forgetting symptoms, questions, or important details when under pressure during a consultation
- Memory lapses due to stress or anxiety in medical settings

### 📝 Disorganization
- Difficulty articulating complex symptoms clearly
- Challenges in presenting a coherent timeline of events
- Struggling to prioritize concerns effectively

### ⏰ Time Pressure
- Feeling rushed by doctors during consultations
- Limited consultation time leading to incomplete communication
- Pressure to remember everything in the moment

### 💊 Medication Confusion
- Forgetting current medications or dosages
- Inability to recall medication history accurately
- Confusion about drug interactions or side effects

### 🤐 Lack of Empowerment
- Feeling unprepared or intimidated in medical settings
- Leading to passive participation in their own healthcare
- Missing opportunities to advocate for their health needs

## Indirect Impact on Healthcare Providers

### ⏱️ Inefficient Consultations
- Doctors spending more time eliciting basic information
- Less time available for diagnosis and treatment planning
- Consultation time not optimized for medical decision-making

### ❓ Incomplete Information
- Patients not providing all relevant details
- Potentially affecting diagnostic accuracy
- Important symptoms or concerns going unmentioned

### 😤 Patient Frustration
- Patients feeling unheard during consultations
- Concerns that their issues weren't fully addressed
- Reduced patient satisfaction with healthcare experience

## Sri Lankan Healthcare Context
- High patient volume in public healthcare system
- Limited consultation time per patient
- Need for efficient, effective patient-doctor communication
- Growing emphasis on patient empowerment in healthcare

## Impact of the Problem
- **Medical:** Potential for misdiagnosis or incomplete treatment
- **Economic:** Inefficient use of healthcare resources
- **Social:** Patient dissatisfaction and reduced trust in healthcare
- **Personal:** Patients feeling helpless in managing their health
