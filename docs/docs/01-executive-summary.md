# Executive Summary

## Project: PatientPrep SL - Your Smart Consultation Companion

**PatientPrep SL** is a mobile application designed to empower patients in Sri Lanka to have more effective and informed medical consultations. It acts as a personal digital assistant for patients before and during a doctor's visit, helping them organize symptoms, compile questions, list medications, and prepare relevant personal health notes.

## Core Value Proposition

The core value is to enable patients to articulate their concerns clearly and comprehensively, leading to more efficient and productive interactions with their doctors. 

## Key Differentiators

### Privacy-First Approach
- **Crucially for MVP:** All user-inputted personal health data (symptoms, notes, medications) is stored **exclusively on the user's device** and not transmitted to a server or shared with doctors via the app
- Maximum privacy with minimal regulatory overhead

### Intelligent Guidance
- An intelligent agentic AI guides patients through the preparation process
- Suggests common questions or relevant points based on general symptom categories
- Helps patients organize their thoughts for the consultation

## Development Context

**Developer:** Individual university student  
**Timeline:** 3-month development cycle following initial 2-week Sprint 0  
**Target:** Lean Minimum Viable Product (MVP) launch on Google Play Store  
**Focus:** Validating the concept of improving consultation quality through patient preparation

## Strategic Approach

Operating entirely on the patient's device for:
- Maximum privacy protection
- Minimal regulatory overhead
- Focus on core value validation
- Reduced technical complexity for solo developer

The initial focus will be on validating the concept of improving consultation quality through patient preparation, with all functionality contained within the patient's mobile device.
